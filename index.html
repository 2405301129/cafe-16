Our Story

More Than Just a Canteen

Born inside the heart of the university, Cafe 16 started as a simple canteen counter and grew into the campus's favourite hangout. We believe every student deserves great food, real coffee, and a space that feels like home between lectures.

From early-morning espressos to late-night study fuel, we're here whenever you need us. Our menu is crafted for student life — affordable, delicious, and always fresh.
